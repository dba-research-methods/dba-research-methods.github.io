> **Bridging Business Problems with Academic Rigor for Executive Doctoral Scholars**
>
> 🌐 **Live Website & Interactive Reference Desk:** [https://dba-research-methods.github.io](https://dba-research-methods.github.io)

## 🏛️ About This Repository

The **DBA Executive Research Reference Desk** is an evidence-based reference application and knowledge system designed specifically for senior business executives (20+ years of corporate leadership) pursuing a **Doctor of Business Administration (DBA)**.

Unlike traditional PhD candidates who focus primarily on abstract theoretical debates, DBA scholars focus on **pragmatic, practice-led inquiry**: solving real-world organizational friction and generating actionable knowledge with academic rigor.

---

## 🧭 Core Architecture & Features

### 1. 🧭 Business Problem Solver
An interactive decision matrix mapping real corporate operational challenges directly to doctoral research designs:
* **Internal Operational Interventions:** Insider Action Research (*David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick*)
* **Multi-Firm Benchmarking & Turnarounds:** Comparative Multi-Case Studies (*Robert K. Yin*) & fsQCA
* **Customer / Employee Perception Modeling:** Partial Least Squares Path Modeling (*Joseph F. Hair et al.*)
* **Digital Transformation Roadmaps:** Design Science Research (*Hevner & Gregor*)
* **Leadership & Organizational Culture:** Grounded Theory & Inductive Thematic Analysis (*Gioia Methodology*)
* **Policy & Strategic Interventions:** Quasi-Experimental Evaluation (*Shadish, Cook & Campbell*)

### 2. 📚 The Executive Bookshelf
Plain-language executive translations of foundational research texts with direct, verified Amazon and academic companion links:
* **Mark Saunders et al.** – *Research Methods for Business Students* (The Research Onion)
* **David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick** – *Doing Action Research in Your Own Organization*
* **Joseph F. Hair et al.** – *A Primer on Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)*
* **Robert K. Yin** – *Case Study Research and Applications: Design and Methods*
* **Matthew B. Miles, A. Michael Huberman & Johnny Saldaña** – *Qualitative Data Analysis*
* **Andy Field** – *Discovering Statistics Using IBM SPSS / R*
* **John W. Creswell** – *Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches*

### 3. 🛠️ Verified GitHub Tool Matrix
Curated open-source software rated by **Executive Usability**:
* **🟢 Zero-Code Web GUIs:** [Taguette](https://www.taguette.org) (Qualitative transcript coding), [ASReview](https://asreview.ai) (AI-assisted systematic literature review)
* **🟡 Desktop Apps & Low-Code:** [QualCoder](https://github.com/ccbogel/QualCoder) (Full CAQDAS suite), [Quarto](https://quarto.org) (Doctoral dissertation publishing)
* **🟠 Scripting & Statistics:** [SEMinR](https://github.com/sem-in-r/seminr) (PLS-SEM in R), [Pingouin](https://pingouin-stats.org) (Statistical tests in Python), [DoWhy](https://github.com/py-why/dowhy) (Causal inference)

### 4. 📖 Executive Jargon Buster (Rosetta Stone)
Translates academic terminology into executive corporate analogies:
* **Epistemology:** "What standard of proof do stakeholders demand before acting on data?"
* **Ontology:** "What is the true nature of the business reality you are investigating?"
* **Construct Validity:** "Are your survey questions actually measuring strategic intent, or just noise?"
* **Triangulation:** "Cross-checking findings with at least three independent data streams (financials, interviews, audit logs)."
* **Theoretical Saturation:** "The point in stakeholder interviewing where additional meetings yield zero new insights."

### 5. 🎧 Executive Audio Walkthrough
Includes an integrated 1-minute spoken orientation guiding scholars through the methodology desk with synchronized presentation slides, live transcript, and chapter navigation.

## 🚀 Deployment & Local Usage

This repository is built as a **zero-dependency static web application** that deploys automatically via **GitHub Pages**.

### Run Locally
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/dba-research-methods/dba-research-methods.github.io.git
cd dba-research-methods.github.io

# Open in any modern web browser
open index.html # on macOS
# or
xdg-open index.html # on Linux
# or simply double-click index.html in file explorer
```
