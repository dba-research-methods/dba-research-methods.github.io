INTERNAL PROBLEM SOLVING

Solving an Operational Breakdown in Your Own Company

Method: Insider Action Research (Cyclical: Diagnose → Plan → Act → Evaluate)

Seminal Text: David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick (Doing Action Research in Your Own Organization)
Recommended Tool: Taguette / QualCoder for qualitative coding of internal reflections, meeting minutes, and change logs.
Executive Watchout: Managing role duality (being both a senior manager and researcher) and organizational politics.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Action Research ↗ 💻 Taguette Tool ↗
CROSS-FIRM BENCHMARKING

Comparing Successful vs Failed Corporate Transformations

Method: Comparative Multi-Case Studies (Yin) combined with fuzzy-set QCA (fsQCA)

Seminal Text: Robert K. Yin (Case Study Research and Applications)
Recommended Tool: R QCA Package / SetMethods for configurational causal recipes across 8-20 firms.
Executive Watchout: Ensuring rival explanations are systematically documented and cross-case evidence is triangulated.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Case Study ↗ 📦 CRAN: QCA Package ↗
SURVEY & MODEL TESTING

Testing Customer or Employee Perception & Performance Links

Method: Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)

Seminal Text: Joseph F. Hair, G. Tomas M. Hult, Christian M. Ringle & Marko Sarstedt (A Primer on PLS-SEM)
Recommended Tool: SEMinR (R package) / Pingouin (Python) / SmartPLS
Executive Watchout: Reporting HTMT ratios for discriminant validity and bootstrapping for path significance.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: PLS-SEM ↗ 🌐 Official Companion (pls-sem.net) ↗
FRAMEWORK & ARTIFACT CREATION

Designing & Validating an Enterprise Framework or AI System

Method: Design Science Research (DSR) · Build & Evaluate Cycle (Hevner & Gregor)

Seminal Text: Alan Hevner & Shirley Gregor (Design Science Research in Information Systems)
Recommended Tool: Quarto / Python for prototype testing and structured expert panel Delphi evaluations.
Executive Watchout: Proving the artifact is innovative and rigorously evaluating its organizational utility.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Design Science ↗
QUALITATIVE INQUIRY

Uncovering How C-Suite Executives Make Critical Choices

Method: Inductive Thematic Analysis / Gioia Methodology (1st order concepts → 2nd order themes → aggregate dimensions)

Seminal Text: Miles, Huberman & Saldaña (Qualitative Data Analysis)
Recommended Tool: ASReview (AI literature triage) & QualCoder / Taguette (interview coding)
Executive Watchout: Demonstrating theoretical saturation across in-depth semi-structured interviews.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Thematic Analysis ↗ 🧠 ASReview Portal ↗
POLICY & STRATEGY EVALUATION

Measuring the True Causal Impact of an Executive Initiative

Method: Quasi-Experimental Field Design (Difference-in-Differences / Propensity Score Matching)

Seminal Text: Shadish, Cook & Campbell (Experimental and Quasi-Experimental Designs for Causal Inference)
Recommended Tool: DoWhy (Python Causal AI) / Pingouin
Executive Watchout: Controlling for confounding historical events and pre-intervention trend differences.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Quasi-Experiment ↗ 📚 DoWhy Documentation ↗
EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS

Systematic Evidence Mapping of Existing Business Research

Method: Systematic Literature Review & Evidence Synthesis (PRISMA 2020 Protocol)

Seminal Guide: PRISMA Statement & Tranfield et al. (Towards a Methodology for Evidence-Informed Management).
Recommended Tool: ASReview Lab (Active learning screening) + Connected Papers (co-citation mapping).
Executive Watchout: Defining explicit search strings, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and quality appraisal checklists.
📋 PRISMA Statement Portal ↗ 🧠 ASReview Lab ↗ 🕸️ Connected Papers ↗

The Executive Bookshelf · Foundational Literature

Seminal research methods texts translated into clear executive takeaways with 1-click citation copying and verified links.

METHODOLOGY BLUEPRINT

Research Methods for Business Students (8th / 9th Edition)

Mark Saunders, Philip Lewis, Adrian Thornhill (Pearson)

Core Framework: The "Research Onion" — peel through Philosophy → Approach → Strategy → Choices → Time Horizons → Data Collection.
Executive Translation: Aligning your worldview (Pragmatism), your logical direction (Inductive vs Deductive), and your tactical execution without methodological contradictions.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Research Design ↗ 🌐 Management Science ↗
PRACTITIONER INQUIRY

Doing Action Research in Your Own Organization (5th Edition)

David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick (SAGE)

Core Framework: The Action Research Cycle (Diagnosing → Planning Action → Taking Action → Evaluating Action).
Executive Translation: How to study an organizational change initiative in your own company without falling into consulting bias.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Action Research ↗
QUANTITATIVE MODELING

A Primer on PLS-SEM (3rd Edition)

Joseph F. Hair, G. Tomas M. Hult, Christian M. Ringle, Marko Sarstedt (SAGE)

Core Framework: Measurement model evaluation (convergent/discriminant validity via HTMT) and Structural model evaluation (R², f², Q²).
Executive Translation: Statistically validating multi-variable causal frameworks (e.g. Employee Culture → Innovation → EBITDA).
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 🌐 Official Companion Site ↗ 📄 HTMT Criterion Paper ↗
QUALITATIVE & COMPARATIVE

Case Study Research and Applications (6th Edition)

Robert K. Yin (SAGE)

Core Framework: 4 Design Tests: Construct Validity, Internal Validity, External Validity (analytic generalization), and Reliability.
Executive Translation: Conducting bulletproof case studies where findings generalize to theory rather than statistical populations.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Case Study ↗
DATA DISPLAY & CODING

Qualitative Data Analysis: A Methods Sourcebook (4th Edition)

Matthew B. Miles, A. Michael Huberman, Johnny Saldaña (SAGE)

Core Framework: Qualitative data condensation, data display (matrices, networks), and drawing/verifying conclusions.
Executive Translation: Organizing hundreds of pages of executive interview transcripts into structured cross-case matrices.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Qualitative Research ↗
STATISTICAL REASONING

Discovering Statistics Using IBM SPSS / R (5th Edition)

Andy Field (SAGE)

Core Framework: Practical inferential statistics, assumptions testing (normality, homoscedasticity), ANOVA, and multiple regression.
Executive Translation: Plain-language, humorous explanations of p-values, effect sizes, and avoiding statistical misinterpretations.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 🌐 Author Companion Site ↗
MIXED METHODS COMPANION

Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, & Mixed Methods

John W. Creswell & J. David Creswell (SAGE)

Core Framework: Tripartite model connecting philosophical worldviews, research designs, and specific inquiry methods.
Executive Translation: Designing mixed-methods studies (e.g. exploratory interviews followed by a quantitative validation survey).
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Research Design ↗
CAUSAL INFERENCE

Experimental & Quasi-Experimental Designs

William R. Shadish, Thomas D. Cook, Donald T. Campbell (Cengage)

Core Framework: Comprehensive taxonomy of 33 threats to validity (history, maturation, selection bias, regression to the mean).
Executive Translation: Isolating the genuine impact of leadership decisions from macroeconomic trends.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Quasi-Experiment ↗
MULTIVARIATE STATISTICS

Multivariate Data Analysis (8th Edition)

Joseph F. Hair, William C. Black, Barry J. Babin, Rolph E. Anderson (Cengage)

Core Framework: Step-by-step decision trees for Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA), MANOVA, Multiple Discriminant Analysis, and Cluster Analysis.
Executive Translation: Uncovering latent dimensions in corporate survey data and grouping customers or business units into empirical clusters.
🛒 Amazon Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Multivariate Analysis ↗

Jargon Translated · Plain English Guide

Dense academic methodology concepts demystified into intuitive corporate leadership analogies.

Epistemology

Wiki ↗
"What standard of evidence is required before we accept a claim as true?"
Executive Analogy: A CFO demanding audited financial ledger entries (Positivism) vs a CEO conducting in-depth stakeholder interviews to understand morale (Interpretivism).

Ontology

Wiki ↗
"What is the true nature of the business reality we are investigating?"
Executive Analogy: Is customer brand loyalty an objective metric that exists independently (Objectivism), or is it socially created through perceptions and relationships (Constructivism)?

Construct Validity

Wiki ↗
"Are your survey questions actually measuring strategic intent or just noise?"
Executive Analogy: Ensuring your employee satisfaction survey isn't just measuring how much staff like the cafeteria coffee instead of their true cultural engagement.

Triangulation

Wiki ↗
"Cross-checking a key insight using at least three distinct data sources."
Executive Analogy: Validating customer churn by cross-referencing Salesforce CRM logs, customer exit interviews, and quarterly financial revenue reports.

Theoretical Saturation

Wiki ↗
"The point where conducting further interviews reveals zero new concepts."
Executive Analogy: After speaking with 18 supply chain VPs, the 19th and 20th interviews only repeat the exact same pain points. You have reached diminishing returns.

HTMT Ratio (< 0.85 / 0.90)

DOI Paper ↗
"The gold standard test proving two business concepts are truly distinct."
Executive Analogy: Mathematically proving that 'Brand Trust' and 'Customer Satisfaction' in your survey are two separate commercial drivers, not the same question asked twice.

Confounding Variable

Wiki ↗
"A hidden third factor that secretly causes both your cause and effect."
Executive Analogy: Claiming your new sales training boosted Q4 revenue, while ignoring that the entire industry grew by 25% due to holiday seasonality.

Action Research Cycle

Wiki ↗
"Simultaneously executing an organizational intervention and researching its impact."
Executive Analogy: Rolling out an enterprise ERP platform inside your own business and rigorously documenting behavioral resistance and workflow adaptations across 4 quarters.

Quasi-Experiment

Wiki ↗
"An empirical study evaluating an intervention without randomized lab assignment."
Executive Analogy: Testing a new performance bonus in your Chicago regional division while using your Dallas division as an un-treated control benchmark.

Gioia Methodology

Wiki ↗
"A systematic 3-tier inductive coding structure for qualitative management research."
Executive Analogy: Aggregating raw executive quotes (1st order) into management themes (2nd order) and overarching strategic theoretical dimensions.
