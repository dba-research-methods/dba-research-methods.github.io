Internal Process Intervention

Solving an Operational Breakdown in Your Own Company

"How can we redesign our enterprise supply chain governance to eliminate cross-functional silos?"

Method: Insider Action Research (Diagnosing → Planning → Acting → Evaluating)
Textbook: David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick (Doing Action Research)
Recommended Tool: taguette (Web CAQDAS) or QualCoder
Key Deliverable: Documented intervention log & organizational change framework.

Survey & Model Testing

Testing Customer or Employee Perception & Performance Links

"Does psychological safety in engineering teams mediate the impact of agile leadership on product velocity?"

Method: Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)
Textbook: Hair, Hult, Ringle & Sarstedt (A Primer on PLS-SEM)
Recommended Tool: sem-in-r/seminr (R) or pingouin (Python)
Key Deliverable: Validated survey scale & path coefficient diagram ($p < 0.05$).
Wiki: PLS-SEM ↗ Amazon Book ↗ SEMinR Repository ↗
Cross-Firm Benchmarking

Comparing Successful vs Failed Corporate Transformations

"Why did 4 healthcare providers successfully adopt AI diagnostics while 4 comparable peers stalled?"

Method: Multiple-Case Study Design & Cross-Case Pattern Matching
Textbook: Robert K. Yin (Case Study Research & Applications)
Recommended Tool: cran/QCA (Fuzzy-set Comparative Analysis)
Key Deliverable: Multi-case cross-comparison matrix & causal configuration table.
Wiki: Case Studies ↗ Amazon Book ↗ CRAN QCA Package ↗
Executive Decision-Making

Uncovering How C-Suite Executives Make Critical Choices

"How do Fortune 500 CFOs weigh geopolitical risk when reallocating capital investments?"

Method: Semi-Structured Qualitative In-Depth Interviews & Thematic Analysis
Textbook: Miles, Huberman & Saldaña (Qualitative Data Analysis)
Recommended Tool: ccbogel/QualCoder (Transcript Coding)
Key Deliverable: Executive interview codebook, thematic hierarchy, & quote matrix.

Program & Policy ROI

Measuring the True Causal Impact of an Executive Training Initiative

"Did our $2M leadership program actually reduce middle-manager turnover, or was it just market hiring trends?"

Method: Quasi-Experimental Evaluation & Difference-in-Differences (DiD)
Textbook: Shadish, Cook & Campbell (Experimental & Quasi-Experimental Designs)
Recommended Tool: py-why/dowhy (Microsoft Causal Inference)
Key Deliverable: Propensity-matched causal effect estimate & refutation tests.
Wiki: Quasi-Experiment ↗ Amazon Book ↗ DoWhy Docs ↗

Systematic Evidence Mapping of Existing Business Research

"What does 15 years of published empirical evidence say about ESG ratings and firm cost of capital?"

Foundational Textbooks Translated for Business Leaders

Click directly into Amazon searches, Wikipedia theoretical overviews, official companion portals, and seminal papers.

Research Methods for Business Students (8th / 9th Edition)

Mark Saunders, Philip Lewis, & Adrian Thornhill (Pearson Education)
The DBA Bible
Executive Translation: The "Research Onion" guarantees your methodology is cohesive. If your philosophy is pragmatic, your business strategy, data collection instruments, and time horizons must logically align.
Layer 1: Philosophy
Pragmatism (what works to solve the problem), Critical Realism (underlying structures behind observable business KPIs).
Layer 2: Approach
Deductive (testing existing management theory) vs Inductive (building new managerial frameworks from data).
Layer 3: Strategy
Action Research, Case Study, Survey, Grounded Theory, Archival Business Analysis.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Research Design ↗ 🌐 Management Science Overview ↗

Doing Action Research in Your Own Organization (5th Edition)

David Coghlan & Teresa Brannick (SAGE Publications)
Insider Research
Executive Translation: Written specifically for senior professionals conducting research inside their own company. Teaches how to manage dual roles (executive authority vs objective researcher) and navigate internal company politics.
The Action Research Cycle
1. Constructing → 2. Planning Action → 3. Taking Action → 4. Evaluating Action → Repeating.
Managing Politics & Ethics
Securing genuine organizational access, managing confidentiality, and preventing confirmation bias.
Scholarly Contribution
Generating actionable knowledge that solves the immediate business crisis while contributing generalizable insights.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Action Research ↗

A Primer on Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)

Joseph F. Hair, G. Tomas M. Hult, Christian M. Ringle, & Marko Sarstedt (SAGE)
Business Surveys
Executive Translation: Why PLS-SEM dominates business journals: unlike old-school regression, it handles complex mediating/moderating models, smaller sample sizes ($N=100-300$), and non-normally distributed customer data.
Measurement Validation
Proof that your survey scale is reliable: Factor Loadings $>0.708$, $AVE > 0.50$, $CR > 0.70$, and HTMT ratio $<0.85$.
Structural Path Testing
Determining which strategic drivers truly move the needle: Path coefficients ($\beta$), $R^2$ variance explained, and $f^2$ effect sizes.
Mediation Analysis
Proving indirect effects (e.g. Leadership $\rightarrow$ Culture $\rightarrow$ Financial Performance) via 5,000 bootstrap resamples.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 🌐 Official Book Companion & Datasets (pls-sem.net) ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: PLS Path Modeling ↗ 📄 Seminal HTMT Validity Paper (JAIS) ↗

Case Study Research and Applications: Design and Methods (6th Edition)

Robert K. Yin (SAGE Publications)
Case Studies
Executive Translation: A business case study is not just an anecdotal story. Yin provides the rigorous blueprint: triangulating multiple data sources (emails, board minutes, interviews) to withstand academic scrutiny.
Construct Validity
Using multiple sources of evidence to establish an unbroken chain of custody for your findings.
Replication Logic
Treating multiple organizational cases like multiple laboratory experiments (predicting similar or contrasting outcomes).
Pattern Matching
Comparing observed operational outcomes against theoretical predictions to rule out rival explanations.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Case Study Method ↗

Qualitative Data Analysis: A Methods Sourcebook (4th Edition)

Matthew B. Miles, A. Michael Huberman, & Johnny Saldaña (SAGE)
Interview Coding
Executive Translation: The definitive guide to organizing 30+ executive interviews: how to code quotes into structured categories, build comparison matrices, and draw defensible conclusions.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Qualitative Research ↗

Discovering Statistics Using IBM SPSS Statistics / R (5th Edition)

Andy Field (SAGE Publications)
Applied Stats
Executive Translation: The most accessible statistics book for non-mathematicians. Focuses on practical decision trees: which statistical test to run, how to check assumptions, and how to interpret business effect sizes.
🛒 Amazon Book Search ↗ 🌐 DiscoveringStatistics.com (Official Companion) ↗ 📖 Wikipedia: Andy Field ↗

Executive Jargon Buster & Wikipedia Portals

Translating academic research terms into corporate executive analogies with verified Wikipedia deep links.

Epistemology Wiki ↗
"What standards of proof and evidence do you demand before believing a claim?"
Executive Analogy: An auditor demanding hard financial ledgers (Positivism) vs a strategist conducting stakeholder interviews (Interpretivism).
Ontology Wiki ↗
"What is the true nature of the business reality you are studying?"
Executive Analogy: Is customer loyalty an objective measurable number (Objectivism) or a perception shaped by social interactions (Constructivism)?
Construct Validity Wiki ↗
"Are your survey questions actually measuring what you intended to measure?"
Executive Analogy: Ensuring your 'Employee Morale' survey isn't just measuring how much people like the office coffee.
Triangulation Wiki ↗
"Cross-checking a finding using at least 3 distinct data sources."
Executive Analogy: Validating a drop in customer satisfaction by checking CRM ticket logs, exit interviews, and financial churn rates together.
Theoretical Saturation Wiki ↗
"The point where conducting further interviews reveals zero new insights."
Executive Analogy: Interviewing 20 enterprise buyers and noticing that buyers #16 through #20 report the exact same pricing complaints.
HTMT Ratio JAIS Paper ↗
"Proof that two survey concepts are distinctly separate and not measuring the same thing."
Executive Analogy: Proving that 'Brand Trust' and 'Brand Awareness' are statistically distinct in your customer survey ($HTMT < 0.85$).
Confounding Variable Wiki ↗
"An unmeasured external factor that creates a misleading correlation."
Executive Analogy: Believing a new marketing campaign drove sales, when an industry-wide interest rate cut was the true driver.
Action Research Wiki ↗
"Simultaneously taking organizational action and doing research on the action."
Executive Analogy: Rolling out a new CRM system inside your own firm and methodically analyzing how teams adapt over 4 quarters.
